Gianmarco Canato

Bassoonist, electronic musician and improviser, based in Varese

Biography

Gianmarco Canato trained as a classical bassoonist and orchestral musician before moving into electronic music. Today he works with bassoon and live electronics across classical music, free improvisation and experimental music theatre.

His practice sits between the fully improvised and the fully composed. It is close to the Roman tradition of improvisation associated with Giancarlo Schiaffini, Musica Elettronica Viva and Alvin Curran, where freedom can coexist with structures and earlier decisions.

He is a doctoral researcher at Conservatories of Alessandria and Como, and an associate researcher at the Bern University of the Arts (HKB). He teaches History of Electroacoustic Music at the Civica Scuola di Musica Claudio Abbado in Milan.
