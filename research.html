Research

Research question

How does approaching my improvisational practice through experimental music theatre shape the way I act through the means each performance makes available?

The project

I start from two means of action, bassoon and live electronics, to which text, images and movement are added from one performance to another. Each dramaturgical framework makes these means available in a different way. The research follows what happens to my decisions and actions as an improviser in each case.

The work is practice-based and draws on rehearsal and performance diaries, video documentation and a set of case studies, each with a different dramaturgical framework.

Doctoral researcher at the Conservatories of Alessandria and Como. Associate researcher at the Bern University of the Arts (HKB).
