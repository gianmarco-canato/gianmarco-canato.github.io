Selected works & research

Bassoon and live electronics between classical music, free improvisation and experimental music theatre.

I am a bassoonist, electronic musician and improviser based in Varese. My practice and doctoral research look at how an improvising musician acts through the means a performance makes available: instrument, electronics, movement, text and image.
